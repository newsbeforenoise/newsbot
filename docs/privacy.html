Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

News Before Noise (@NewsB4Noise) is an automated news-headline service. It publishes short headlines and summary videos to our own accounts on Telegram, Threads, X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data about readers or viewers. We have no sign-up, no tracking cookies and no analytics on this website.

Google / YouTube data

Our app "NBN Uploader" uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to our own YouTube channel (News Before Noise), with the youtube.upload permission. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google user data, and it is not used by anyone other than News Before Noise. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uses of YouTube API Services are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Other platforms

When you view our posts on Telegram, Threads, X, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube, those platforms' own privacy policies apply.

Deleting data

We hold no personal data about you. To revoke the NBN Uploader's access to a Google account, visit Google Account permissions.

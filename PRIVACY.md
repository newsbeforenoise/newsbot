# Privacy Policy — News Before Noise (NBN Poster / NBN Threads)

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

**News Before Noise** is an automated headline service. Its apps ("NBN Poster" and
"NBN Threads") are used only by the owner of News Before Noise to publish headline
posts to the News Before Noise accounts on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Telegram,
Bluesky and other platforms.

## What data we collect
- **None from the public.** The apps do not log in other users, do not read anyone's
  profile, messages, comments, contacts or followers, and do not collect personal data.
- The apps act only on accounts owned by News Before Noise, using access tokens stored
  privately by the owner.

## What the apps do
- Read public news headlines (RSS feeds) and public market prices.
- Publish short text posts and image cards to News Before Noise's own accounts.

## Sharing and selling
We do not sell, share or transfer any personal data, because we do not collect any.

## Data deletion
There is no user data to delete. If you believe any data about you is held, or you want
a post removed, contact us and we will respond within 30 days:
**newsbeforenoisesree@gmail.com**
